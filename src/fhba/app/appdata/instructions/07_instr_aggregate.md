## Aggregate Burn Scars

This step unions all finalized burn masks for the selected year and satellite into a single **seasonal burn map**.

Click `Generate Seasonal Burn Map` to create the aggregated GeoTIFF. This combines all dates that were successfully classified in the previous step.

Once generated, click `Compute County Area Statistics` to calculate burned area in km² and acres for each county in the study area.

Use the `Download Statistics CSV` button to save the per-county results.
