## True Color Image Previews

Use the media slider to the right to preview the true-color images downloaded in the previous step. For each date, update the `User Categorization` from `Uncategorized` to one of the following categories:
* `Fully Clear`
* `Mostly Clear`
* `Mostly Cloudy`
* `Fully Cloudy`

Once all dates have been categorized, click `Save User Categorizations` and then click `Next` to continue the analysis.
