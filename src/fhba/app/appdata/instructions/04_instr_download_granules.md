## Download Satellite Granules

Select one or more `User Categorizations` to download granules for, then authenticate with NASA Earthdata and click `Download Granules for Selected Categories`.

**Authentication**

Enter your [NASA Earthdata](https://urs.earthdata.nasa.gov/) username and password and click `Authenticate NASA Earthdata` before downloading.

**Category Selection**

Only dates matching the selected categorizations will be downloaded. It is recommended to download only `Fully Clear` and `Mostly Clear` dates to reduce processing time.

**Download Progress**

Progress is shown in the progress bar and download log below the button. Downloading and processing granules can take several minutes per date — please be patient.

Once all desired granules have been downloaded, click `Next` to continue the analysis.
