## Identify Burned and Unburned Pixels

Select a date for analysis from the dropdown to the right and click `Load Analysis Date Image`. 

When the image loads in the pane to the right use the two `point draw tools` <img src="../_static/pointdrawtoolicon.png" width=15px/> to select burned and unburned points. Burned pixels are shown as red X's and unburned pixels are shown as white crosses. 

- *To place a point*: Click on the point draw tool and select a location on the image

- *To move a point*: Click and drag an existing point to a new location 

- *To delete a point*: Click an existing point and press the `BACKSPACE` key 

__Once points have been selected__ click the red `export points` button and add points for a new image or click `next` to proceed to the categorization step.
