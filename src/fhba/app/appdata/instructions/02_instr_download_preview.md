## Download Preview Images

Use the sliders to the right to specify the beginning and ending dates and click `Download Preview Images` to download the images. These true-color images will be acquired by downloading stills images from __[NASA Worldview](https://worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov/)__ based on the user's specified latitude and longitude limits. 

The most recent downloaded image will be displayed in the space below the progress bar. When looping through days, if an image is already present the image download will be skipped for the current day. 

Once all images have been downloaded, click `Next` to continue the analysis.
