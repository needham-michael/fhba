# Instruments and Satellites

The application currently supports imagery from the Visible Infrared Imaging Radiometer Suite (__[VIIRS](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/instruments/viirs)__) onboard the AQUA and TERRA satellites, the Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer (__[MODIS](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/instruments/modis)__) onboard the Suomi-NPP and JPSS series satellites, and the Ocean and Land Colour Instrument (__[OLCI](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/instruments/olci)__) onboard the Sentinel-3 series satellites operated by the European Space Agency (ESA).

These instruments are imaging spectrometers onboard polar-orbiting satellites.

## Satellite Platforms

| Platform | Instrument | Start Date | End Date |
| -------- | ---------- | ---------- | -------- |
| Terra | MODIS | 24 February 2000 | February 2027[^modis] |
| Aqua | MODIS | 4 July 2002 | September 2027[^modis] |
| Suomi-NPP | VIIRS | 19 January 2012 | November 1, 2026[^snpp] |
| NOAA-20 | VIIRS | 5 January 2018 | - |
| NOAA-21 | VIIRS | 10 February 2023 | - |
| NOAA-22 | VIIRS | *expected 2027*[^jpss] | - |
| NOAA-23 | VIIRS | *expected 2032*[^jpss] | - |
| Sentinel-3A | OLCI | 16 February 2016 | - |
| Sentinel-3B | OLCI | 25 April 2016 | - |
| Sentinel-3C[^3c] | OLCI | 15 September 2026 | - |

[^modis]: The Terra and Aqua platforms are expected to shutdown in late 2026/early 2027. For more information see __[
Transition from MODIS to VIIRS (NASA Earthdata)](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/alerts-outages/transition-from-modis-viirs)__
[^snpp]: On August 7, 2026 it was announced the NASA will cease delivery of data from instruments aboard Suomi-NPP at 13:00 UTC on November 1, 2026. For more information see __[Suomi NPP Data Product Delivery to Cease on November 1, 2026 (NASA Earthdata)](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/alerts-outages/suomi-npp-data-product-delivery-cease-november-1-2026)__
[^jpss]: The next satellites in NOAA's Joint Polar Satellite System (JPSS) are in development for launch readiness in 2027 and 2032. For more information see __[Joint Polar Satellite System (NOAA)](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/our-satellites/currently-flying/joint-polar-satellite-system)__ and __[JPSS (NASA)](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/jpss/)__
[^3c]: Sentinel-3C will be added at a future date when data becomes available
